# The reinvention of BlackBerry

# 黑莓的革新

> **Source**: [WeChat](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/jlmv8GPri0HyTPM0zQA62A)

---

## 📉 Decline

BlackBerry, the Canadian manufacturer of a once-iconic messaging accessory used by business people with dextrous thumbs, has been in decline for more than a decade.

> 黑莓是加拿大制造商，是拥有灵巧拇指的商业人士们曾使用的标志性配件，十多年来一直在走下坡路。

Its reluctance to replace the devices' fiddly buttons with touchscreens, and its failure to embrace apps, ceded the smartphone market to Apple and Android.

> 黑莓不愿将他们设备上繁琐的按钮换成触屏，也未能及时接受应用程序，将智能手机市场让给了苹果和安卓。

The firm stopped making its handsets in **2020**, and finally ceased support for them **this year**.

> 该公司于2020年停止生产其手机，并在今年最终停止了对它们的支持。

---

## 🔄 Reinvention

Since then it has tried to reinvent itself as a maker of **business software**.

> 从那时起开始，它一直试图将自己重塑为商业软件制造商。

In its quarterly results due on Tuesday, analysts hope for evidence of a successful turnaround.

> 在周二发布的季度报告中，分析人士希望提供一个成功转亏为盈的证据。

---

## 📊 Current Business

### 🔐 Cybersecurity

BlackBerry's cybersecurity business, including antivirus software and secure mobile apps, is growing **slowly but steadily**.

> 黑莓的网络安全业务，包括防病毒软件和安全手机应用程序，正在缓慢但稳定的增长。

### 📡 Internet of Things (IoT)

**More promising** is its booming IoT business. BlackBerry software is embedded in:
- **215 million vehicles**
- Various other bits of machinery

> 更有前景的是其蓬勃发展的物联网业务；黑莓软件嵌入在2.15亿辆汽车和其他各种机械设备中。

---

## 💰 Profitability

> *Could it eventually turn a profit?*

**One day, perhaps** — but expect it to announce another loss-making quarter first.

> 最终能否盈利？也许有一天会，但预计他会首先宣布另一个季度的亏损。
