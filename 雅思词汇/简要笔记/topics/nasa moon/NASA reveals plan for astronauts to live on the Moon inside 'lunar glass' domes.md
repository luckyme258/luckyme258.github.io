# NASA reveals plan for astronauts to live on the Moon inside 'lunar glass' domes

Source: [LADbible](https://www.ladbible.com/news/science/nasa-announces-plans-moon-base-2032-186355-20260526)

An ambitious plan to build 'lunar glass' domes on the surface of the Moon has received backing from NASA.

NASA is funding a project aiming to develop glass domes which can be created on the Moon. Spearheaded by California-based company Skyeports, the plan would utilise particles known as 'lunar regolith' found on the moon's surface to create large glass domes under which astronauts could live.

"Space has always been kind of fascinating for me, and my background was as an architect so I had the chance to start exploring how we can build structures on the Moon or Mars," said Skyeports chief executive Dr Martin Bermudez.

"I contacted NASA almost two years ago and they really loved the idea from the beginning."

The team have since revealed it's possible to use lunar regolith to create glass spheres.

**How would the domes be built on the Moon?**  

Astronauts would gather regolith, which contains glass-making properties known as silicates, and feed it into a microwave furnace transported to the lunar surface. The furnace would then blow glass bubbles.

Researchers are also developing a special type of glass made from polymers, allowing the bubbles to 'self-heal' and withstand the harsh environment on the Moon's surface.

Inside the structure will be a habitat allowing astronauts to grow food in living quarters with both work and recreational spaces.

Skyeports' idea is currently being researched as part of NASA's Innovative Advanced Concepts (NIAC) programme.
