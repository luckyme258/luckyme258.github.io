# NASA reveals plan for astronauts to live on the Moon inside 'lunar glass' domes

> **Source**: [LADbible](https://www.ladbible.com/news/science/nasa-announces-plans-moon-base-2032-186355-20260526)

---

## 📌 Overview

An ambitious plan to build **'lunar glass' domes** on the surface of the Moon has received backing from **NASA**.

NASA is funding a project aiming to develop glass domes which can be created on the Moon.

---

## 🚀 The Project

Spearheaded by California-based company **Skyeports**, the plan would utilise particles known as **'lunar regolith'** (dust, rock, and mineral fragments coating the Moon's surface) to create large glass domes under which astronauts could live.

> 💬 *"Space has always been kind of fascinating for me, and my background was as an architect so I had the chance to start exploring how we can build structures on the Moon or Mars."*  
> — **Dr Martin Bermudez**, Skyeports CEO

> 💬 *"I contacted NASA almost two years ago and they really loved the idea from the beginning."*

The team have since revealed it's possible to use lunar regolith to create **glass spheres**.

---

## 🔧 How Would the Domes Be Built on the Moon?

1. **Gather regolith** – contains silicates (glass-making properties)
2. **Feed into a microwave furnace** – transported to the lunar surface
3. **Furnace blows glass bubbles** – forming the dome structure

> ✨ Researchers are also developing a special type of glass made from **polymers**, allowing the bubbles to **'self-heal'** and withstand the harsh environment on the Moon's surface.

---

## 🏠 Inside the Dome

Inside the structure will be a **habitat** allowing astronauts to:

- Grow their own food
- Live in comfortable quarters
- Access both **work** and **recreational spaces**

---

## 📡 Current Status

Skyeports' idea is currently being researched as part of **NASA's Innovative Advanced Concepts (NIAC)** programme.
