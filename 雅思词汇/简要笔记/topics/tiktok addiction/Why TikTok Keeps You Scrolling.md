# Why TikTok Keeps You Scrolling: Baylor Research Explains the Science Behind Social Media Addiction

> **Source**: Baylor University Media & Public Relations  
> **Author**: Kelly Craine  
> **Date**: November 5, 2025  
> **Link**: [Original Article](https://news.web.baylor.edu/news/story/2025/why-tiktok-keeps-you-scrolling-baylor-research-explains-science-behind-social-media)

---

## 📌 Key Finding

Short-form video design fuels engagement — and addiction — through **ease, accuracy and surprise**.

> *"It's the combination of all three that keeps people scrolling. But the prerequisite is effortlessness. Without that ease of use, the other two wouldn't matter as much."*  
> — **Meredith E. David, Ph.D.**

---

## 🔬 The Study

A new study by Baylor University marketing professors **James A. Roberts, Ph.D.** and **Meredith E. David, Ph.D.** is among the first to compare the technological affordances (built-in design features) of three leading short-form video platforms:

| Platform | Key Features |
|----------|--------------|
| TikTok | High effortlessness, high accuracy, high surprise |
| Instagram Reels | Moderate scores |
| YouTube Shorts | Moderate scores |

**Results:** TikTok scored significantly higher than competitors across all three categories.

---

## 🎯 Three Key Technological Affordances

### 1️⃣ Perceived Effortlessness
TikTok requires the least effort to use. Videos begin playing automatically the moment the app opens — creating a seamless, immersive experience.

### 2️⃣ Recommendation Accuracy
Users said TikTok delivers the most relevant videos.

### 3️⃣ Serendipity (Element of Surprise)
TikTok surprises users most often with unexpected but enjoyable content.

---

## ⚠️ From Engagement to Addiction

The study found that TikTok's technological affordances indirectly increase addiction by **first increasing engagement**.

The more users engage, the more likely they are to:
- Lose track of time (time distortion)

> *"TikTok's algorithm is intentionally created to be addictive. Their own materials acknowledge that users can become hooked after less than half an hour on the app."*  
> — **Meredith E. David, Ph.D.**

> *"Check your phone's screen-time data — you may be surprised."*

---

## 🌍 Broader Implications

For young people, excessive time on short-form video apps can:
- Erode attention spans
- Foster expectations for instant gratification
- Displace face-to-face interaction

> *"The opportunity cost is huge. The more time we spend scrolling, the less time we have for the activities that build real connection and meaning."*
