## 📌 Introduction

Ever heard of the term **"TikTok brain"**? It is fittingly coined to hint at what the short video platform does to human cognition and overall mental functioning.

Although we still lack comprehensive longitudinal research into the phenomenon, there is piling evidence for the detrimental effects of the app on regular users, especially on the brain development of children and teens.

Here is why TikTok appears to be the **worst social media platform of all**.

---

## 🧠 Drastically reduces memory and attention

Binging on an endless stream of 15-to-30-second-long videos slashes our attention span like no other type of media can.

Short-term memory and the ability to concentrate are also affected. Devoted TikTokers report that they are unable to focus on longer video formats anymore.

- 50% of users admit that they find longer videos **"stressful"**
- In 2022, the optimal length of TikTok videos is estimated to fall between **21 and 34 seconds**

Other social platforms begin to feel "slow" and boring, compared to this fast-paced app.

---

## 🎰 Addictive by design

TikTok's feed is created **addictive by design**.

Unlike Facebook or Instagram that mostly serve content from people or brands we already follow, TikTok revolves around the **endless scroll**, pumping out algorithm-based recommendations from unknown "creators".

> The content-detection algorithm is purposely developed and constantly refined to match users' interests and keep them on the platform for as long as possible.

TikTok operates on the psychological principle of **random reinforcement** — similar to gambling.

**Key research findings:**

| Study | Finding |
|-------|---------|
| 2013 MRI study | Online game addiction leads to structural atrophy in the frontal cortex |
| 2018 research | Visible brain atrophy in children aged 9–10 who use smartphones 7+ hours/day |

---

## 😩 Perpetuates boredom

TikTok has the power to relieve boredom almost instantaneously — but this comes at a price:

- Decreased ability to keep oneself entertained without the app
- Increased dependence on the app

> One study found that proneness to boredom in children and adolescents is linked to increased access to technology, which further fuels digital addiction.

---

## 📚 Affects reading skills

Our brains digest visual information faster than text. TikTok is especially detrimental for reading habits because **the platform does not include text at all**.

---

## ⚠️ Final Note

All "traditional" social media platforms have started copying TikTok's addictive features:

- YouTube → **Shorts**
- Instagram → **Reels**

> Social media is shifting to capture our ever-eroding attention with even smaller bits of easy-to-digest content — and it is up to us users to be aware of all the pitfalls.
