# 🚀 Chapter 1 — The Country Boy Who Never Saw the Sky

---

## 👨‍🎓 Meet Gaal Dornick

| Fact | Detail |
|:---|:---|
| Name | Gaal Dornick |
| Status | Country boy |
| Seen Trantor IRL? | ❌ No |
| Seen Trantor on hyper-video? | ✅ Yes |
| Seen it in 3D news? | ✅ Yes (Imperial Coronation, Galactic Council) |
| Home planet | Synnax (near Blue Drift — edge of galaxy) |

---

His name was Gaal Dornick and he was just a country boy who had never seen Trantor before.

Not in real life, anyway.

He'd seen it on hyper-video. Sometimes in huge 3D newscasts — an Imperial Coronation here, a Galactic Council opening there.

He grew up on Synnax. A planet at the edge of the Blue Drift. Far from everything.

But he wasn't cut off from civilization.

*At that time, no place in the Galaxy was.*

> 🧾 *hyper-video* = future TV
> 🧾 *tremendous* = very large/great
> 🧾 *three-dimensional* = 3D (height, width, depth)
> 🧾 *Coronation* = ceremony where a king/emperor gets crowned
> 🧾 *Council* = group of people who make decisions

---

## 🌍 The Galaxy Then

| Statistic | Number |
|:---|:---|
| Inhabited planets | ~25 million |
| Owe allegiance to Empire? | Every single one |
| Empire's seat (capital) | Trantor |
| Special note | Last half-century this was true |

---

There were nearly twenty-five million inhabited planets in the Galaxy then.

And every single one of them owed allegiance to the Empire.

The Empire's seat was on Trantor.

⭐ *It was the last half‑century in which that could be said.*

> 🧾 *inhabited* = lived on by people
> 🧾 *allegiance* = loyalty to a leader or country
> 🧾 *seat* = the center of government

---

## 🎓 Gaal's Big Trip

| Aspect | Detail |
|:---|:---|
| This trip | The *climax* of his scholarly life |
| Been in space before? | ✅ Yes |
| How far? | To Synnax's satellite (moon) |
| Why? | Data on meteor driftage for his dissertation |
| Distance doesn't matter | Half a million miles = many light years — same thing? |

---

To Gaal, this trip was the undoubted climax of his young, scholarly life.

He'd been in space before — so the *trip* itself wasn't a big deal.

But only as far as Synnax's moon. For his dissertation. Meteor driftage data.

*Still,* he thought, *space is space. Half a million miles or a thousand light years — same thing, right?*

> 🧾 *undoubted* = certain, no question
> 🧾 *climax* = most exciting/important moment
> 🧾 *scholarly* = related to serious studying
> 🧾 *satellite* = a moon or object that orbits a planet
> 🧾 *driftage* = movement of drifting objects
> 🧾 *dissertation* = long university paper

---

## 🌀 The Jump (Hyper-space)

| Question | Answer |
|:---|:---|
| Did Gaal experience Jump before? | ❌ No (only interplanetary trips) |
| Is Jump the only practical method? | ✅ Yes (and probably forever) |
| Ordinary space travel max speed? | Speed of light |
| How long to nearest stars at light speed? | Years |
| Hyper-space travel time? | Between two instants |

> 🧾 *steeled himself* = made himself mentally ready
> 🧾 *phenomenon* = an event you can experience
> 🧾 *interplanetary* = between planets
> 🧾 *proceed* = go/continue
> 🧾 *rate* = speed
> 🧾 *dawn* = beginning
> 🧾 *unimaginable* = impossible to picture
> 🧾 *traverse* = travel across
> 🧾 *interval* = short period between events
> 🧾 *instants* = very short moments

---

He had steeled himself just a little for the Jump through hyper-space.

Never done it before. Only simple interplanetary trips.

The Jump was — and probably *would* be forever — the only practical way to travel between stars.

Regular space? Speed of light. That's it.

Even the nearest star system would take *years*.

But hyper-space?

That strange region that's neither space nor time, matter nor energy, something nor nothing...

Through that, you could cross the *entire Galaxy* in the blink of an eye.

Literally. Between two neighboring instants.

---

## 😌 The First Jump

| Expectation | Reality |
|:---|:---|
| Dread curled in stomach | A tiny jar |
| Fear level: high | A little internal kick |
| — | Stopped before he could be sure he felt it |

---

Gaal had waited for the first Jump with a little dread curled gently in his stomach.

And... nothing.

Just a tiny jar. A little internal kick.

It stopped *before* he could even be sure he felt it.

*That's it?*

---

## 🛸 After the Jump

| What remained | What Gaal had |
|:---|:---|
| The ship — large, glistening | Doctorate in math (fresh) |
| 12,000 years of Imperial progress | Invitation from Hari Seldon |
| — | To join the Seldon Project (vast + mysterious) |

---

And after that...

Just the ship. Large. Glistening.

12,000 years of Imperial progress, all around him.

And himself — fresh doctorate in mathematics — holding an invitation from the *great Hari Seldon*.

Come to Trantor. Join the Seldon Project.

Vast. Mysterious.

*What have I gotten myself into?*

---

## ✨ Waiting for Trantor

| What Gaal wanted | What he did |
|:---|:---|
| First sight of Trantor | Haunted the View-room |
| — | Steel shutter-lids rolled back at announced times |
| — | Watched the stars |

---

What Gaal really waited for — after the disappointment of the Jump — was that first sight of Trantor.

He haunted the View-room.

Every time they announced the steel shutter-lids would roll back — he was there.

Watching the hard brilliance of the stars.

---

## 🌠 What He Saw Out There

| Sight | Description |
|:---|:---|
| Star cluster | Like a giant swarm of fireflies — caught mid-motion, stilled forever |
| Gaseous nebula | Cold, blue-white smoke, 5 light years away |
| On the window | Like distant milk |
| In the room | Icy tinge |
| After 2 hours + another Jump | Gone |

---

He watched the incredible hazy swarm of a star cluster — like a giant conglomeration of fire-flies, caught mid-motion, frozen forever.

Once, there was a nebula. Gaseous. Cold. Blue-white smoke. Five light years away.

It spread across the window like distant milk. The room felt icy.

Two hours later — another Jump — and it was gone.

> 🧾 *haunted* = went to a place again and again
> 🧾 *brilliance* = bright shine
> 🧾 *hazy* = not clear, like through mist
> 🧾 *swarm* = large moving group
> 🧾 *conglomeration* = collection of many things
> 🧾 *gaseous* = made of gas
> 🧾 *nebula* = cloud of gas/dust in space
> 🧾 *tinge* = small amount of a color/feeling

---

## ☀️ Trantor's Sun

| First sight | Progression |
|:---|:---|
| Hard, white speck | Almost lost among millions |
| Recognizable only because guide pointed it out | With each Jump: brighter |
| Stars near Galactic center: thick | Drowning out the rest |

---

The first sight of Trantor's sun?

A hard, white speck. Almost invisible among a *myriad* of others.

The guide had to point it out.

The stars were thick here — near the Galactic center.

But with each Jump, that speck grew brighter.

Drowning out the others. Making them pale. Thinning them out.

> 🧾 *speck* = very small spot
> 🧾 *myriad* = very large number
> 🧾 *recognizable* = able to be identified
> 🧾 *drowning out* = making something hard to see by being stronger
> 🧾 *paling* = becoming lighter/less bright

| 🗣️ Speaker | 💬 Line |
|:---|:---|
| Officer | "View-room will be closed for the remainder of the trip. Prepare for landing." |

---

An officer came through.

"View-room will be closed for the remainder of the trip. Prepare for landing."

## 😬 Gaal's Request

| Gaal's action | Officer's reaction |
|:---|:---|
| Clutched officer's sleeve | Smiled |
| "Would it be possible to let me stay? I would like to see Trantor." | Gaal flushed — realized his provincial accent |

---

Gaal followed him. Clutched his sleeve — the white uniform with the Spaceship-and-Sun of the Empire on it.

"Would it be possible to let me stay? I would like to see Trantor."

The officer smiled.

Gaal flushed.

*Oh no. I sound like a country boy.*

> 🧾 *clutching* = holding tightly
> 🧾 *flushed* = face turned red
> 🧾 *provincial* = from the countryside, not the big city

---

## 🗣️ The Conversation

| Officer | Gaal |
|:---|:---|
| "We'll be landing on Trantor by morning." | "I mean I want to see it from Space." |
| "Sorry, my boy. If this were a space-yacht we might manage it. But we're spinning down, sunside. You wouldn't want to be blinded, burnt, and radiation-scarred all at the same time, would you?" | — |
| "Trantor would only be a gray blur anyway, Kid. Why don't you take a space-tour once you hit Trantor. They're cheap." | "Thank you very much." |

---

"We'll be landing on Trantor by morning," the officer said.

"No — I mean — I want to see it from *Space*."

"Oh. Sorry, my boy. If this were a space-yacht, maybe. But we're spinning down, sunside. You wouldn't want to be blinded, burnt, and radiation-scarred all at the same time, would you?"

Gaal started to walk away.

The officer called after him, "Trantor would only be a gray blur anyway, Kid. Why don't you take a space-tour once you hit Trantor. They're cheap."

Gaal looked back. "Thank you very much."

> 🧾 *yacht* = fancy boat/ship
> 🧾 *radiation-scarred* = damaged by harmful rays

---

## 😔 The Disappointment

| Gaal's feeling | Why |
|:---|:---|
| Childish disappointment | Had never seen Trantor spread out in all its incredibility |
| Lump in throat | As large as life — and now he had to wait longer |

---

It was childish to feel disappointed.

But childishness comes as naturally to a man as to a child.

There was a lump in Gaal's throat.

He had never seen Trantor spread out in all its incredibility — as large as life.

And he hadn't expected to have to wait longer.

> 🧾 *incredibility* = quality of being hard to believe (because so amazing)
> 🧾 *as large as life* = in real size, not a picture
