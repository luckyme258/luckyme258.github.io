# 🎭 The Next Day — Different

---

## 🪑 The Setup

| Before | Now |
|:---|:---|
| Hostile courtroom | Private meeting |
| Audience of Barons | **Alone with the Commission** |
| Separate sides | Seated at the **same table** |
| Formal trial | "No longer a trial" |

> 🧾 *accused* =被告

---

### The Cigars 🚬

- Box: **iridescent plastic** (彩虹色的塑料)
- Appearance: like water, endlessly flowing
- Eyes see motion; fingers feel hard and dry

| Who accepted | Who refused |
|:---|:---|
| Seldon ✅ | Gaal ❌ |

> 🧾 *iridescent* = rainbow-colored, shifting in the light

---

## 🗣️ The New Tone

**Seldon:** "My lawyer is not present."

**Commissioner:** "This is no longer a trial, Dr. Seldon. We are here to discuss the **safety of the State**."

> 🧾 *State* = nation, government

---

## 👑 Linge Chen Speaks

The other Commissioners sit back. Silence forms around Chen.

> *"I will speak."*

---

### Who Is Linge Chen?

| Fact | Detail |
|:---|:---|
| Appearance | Lean and hard |
| Age | Older in looks than in fact |
| True power | **The actual Emperor of all the Galaxy** |
| The child emperor | Just a symbol — manufactured by Chen |

> 🧾 *lean* = thin
> 🧾 *manufactured* = made, produced

*Chen is the power behind the throne.*

---

## 💬 Chen's First Question

> *"None of the quadrillions living now will be alive a century from now. Why should we concern ourselves with events **three centuries** away?"*

> 🧾 *quadrillions* = 10^15 (a thousand trillions)

---

### Seldon's Answer

| What Seldon says | Why |
|:---|:---|
| "I won't be alive in 5 years" | Truth |
| "But it's still of **overpowering concern** to me" | Call it **idealism** |
| Or: identification with **"humanity"** | Mystical generalization |

> 🧾 *overpowering* = overwhelming, too strong to ignore
> 🧾 *idealism* =追求理想，不计现实
> 🧾 *mystical* =神秘的
> 🧾 *generalization* =概括

---

## ⚔️ The Threat

**Chen:** (coldly)

> *"Why may I not rid myself of you — and of this uncomfortable three-century future — by having you executed **tonight**?"*

---

### Seldon's Counter-Threat 💣

| Timeframe | Probability of Chen surviving the year |
|:---|:---|
| A week ago | **1 in 10** (10%) |
| Today | **1 in 10,000** (0.01%) |

*The room stirs. Uncomfortable. Short hairs prickle on Gaal's neck.*

> 🧾 *expired breaths* = breaths let out (relief/surprise)
> 🧾 *uneasy stirrings* = restless, nervous movement
> 🧾 *prickle* =微微刺痛

**Chen's eyelids drop a little.**

> 🧾 *eyelids* =眼皮

---

## 📉 Why the Odds Changed

**Seldon explains:**

> *"The fall of Trantor cannot be stopped. But it can be **hastened easily**."*

| What would happen if Seldon is killed |
|:---|
| Tale of interrupted trial → spreads through Galaxy |
| Frustration of his plan → people lose hope |
| They already envy their grandfathers' lives |
| Political revolutions + trade stagnation → increase |
| Feeling: only what you grab *now* matters |
| Ambitious men don't wait |
| Unscrupulous men don't hang back |
| **Every action hastens decay** |

> 🧾 *hastened* = sped up
> 🧾 *frustration* =失望，挫败
> 🧾 *stagnation* =停滞
> 🧾 *pervade* = spread throughout
> 🧾 *grasp* = seize, grab
> 🧾 *unscrupulous* =不择手段的，无道德的

---

### The Prediction

| If Seldon is killed | Timeframe |
|:---|:---|
| Trantor falls | Not in 300 years — but **within 50 years** |
| Chen dies | **Within 1 year** |

> 🧾 *decay* = decline, rotting

**Chen:** "These are words to frighten children."

*But he doesn't say Seldon is wrong.*

---

## 🤔 Chen's Alternative

> *"Your death is not the only answer that will satisfy us."*

---

### The Question

**Chen:** "Will your only activity be preparing the encyclopedia?"

**Seldon:** "It will."

**Chen:** "Must that be done on Trantor?"

**Seldon:** "Trantor has the Imperial Library and the University."

---

### Chen's Suggestion

> *"What if you were located elsewhere? A planet where the hurry and distractions of a metropolis won't interfere. Where your men can devote themselves **entirely and single-mindedly** to their work."*

> 🧾 *metropolis* = big city
> 🧾 *distractions* =分心的事物
> 🧾 *single-mindedly* =专心地，一心一意地

**Seldon:** "Minor advantages, perhaps."

---

## 🌍 The Chosen World

**Chen:**

> *"The Galaxy will know you are working and fighting the Fall. They will be told you will **prevent** the Fall."*

*(Chen smiles. He doesn't believe in the Fall anyway.)*

> *"You will not trouble Trantor. No disturbance of the Emperor's peace."*

---

### The Two Options

| ✅ Exile | ❌ Death |
|:---|:---|
| Leave Trantor | Execution |
| Take your people | Your followers too |
| Work on your encyclopedia | — |

> *"The opportunity for choosing between death and exile is given you — from this moment to **five minutes** from now."*

---

## 🗺️ The Destination

**Gaal's thought:** *"It is at the edge of the Galaxy!"*

**Chen:** "As I have said — somewhat secluded. It will suit your needs for concentration."

---

## ⏳ The Countdown

**Chen:** "Come. You have **two minutes** left."

**Seldon:** "We will need time to arrange the trip. **Twenty thousand families** are involved."

**Chen:** "You will be given time."

*The last minute begins to die.*

---

## ✅ The Decision

> *"I accept exile."*

— **Hari Seldon**

---

## 💔 Gaal's Mixed Feelings

| Joy | Regret |
|:---|:---|
| Escape death! | Seldon was **defeated** |
| Who wouldn't be relieved? | A little regret remains |

> 🧾 *defeated* =被打败的

## 💭 Think About This

Seldon just got **exiled**.

But he knew this would happen. (He predicted Gaal's arrest. He probably predicted this too.)

Was he *defeated* — or did he get **exactly what he wanted**?

Terminus. The edge of the Galaxy. Far from Trantor. Far from the Commission. *Isolated.*
