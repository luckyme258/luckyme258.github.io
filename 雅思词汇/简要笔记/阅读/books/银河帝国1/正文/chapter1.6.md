# ⚖️ THE TRIAL OF HARI SELDON

## The Day the Empire Listened to Math

---

## 🏛️ The Courtroom

| Feature | Detail |
|:---|:---|
| What Gaal calls it | A trial (barely) |
| Legal resemblance | Almost none |
| Duration | Third day |
| The target | **Hari Seldon** (Gaal = just pecked at) |
| Audience | Barons of the Empire only |
| Press & public | ❌ Excluded |
| Atmosphere | Hostile |

> 🧾 *legalistically* = by strict legal definition
> 🧾 *elaborate* = complex, detailed
> 🧾 *pecked at* = criticized lightly, minor attacks
> 🧾 *unrelieved* = constant, without break

---

## 👨‍⚖️ The Judges

**The Commission of Public Safety** — Five of them.

- Wearing: scarlet and gold uniforms
- Headgear: shining, close-fitting plastic caps (sign of judicial function)
- Center seat: **Chief Commissioner Linge Chen**

> 🧾 *judicial* = related to judging/justice

**Gaal's reaction:** *He had never before seen so great a Lord.*

**Chen's style:** Rarely said a word. Much speech = beneath his dignity.

> 🧾 *dignity* = self-respect, importance

---

## 🗣️ The Examination

*(Seldon on the stand)*

---

### Q1: How many people in your project?

| Seldon says | Advocate says |
|:---|:---|
| 50 mathematicians (51 with Gaal) | Nearly **100,000** in all capacities |
| — | "Ninety-eight thousand, five hundred and seventy-two" |

**Seldon's response:** "I believe you are counting women and children."

**Advocate:** (raising voice) "There is no need to quibble!"

> 🧾 *quibble* = argue about small, unimportant details

**Seldon:** "I accept the figures."

---

### Q2: What about Trantor's future?

**Seldon:** "Trantor will lie in ruins within the next **three centuries**."

**Advocate:** "You don't consider that disloyal?"

**Seldon:** "Scientific truth is beyond loyalty and disloyalty."

> 🧾 *disloyal* = not loyal, betraying

---

### Q3: Can you prove the math is valid?

**Seldon:** "Only to another mathematician."

**Advocate:** (smiling) "So your truth is so *esoteric* that a plain man can't understand it?"

> 🧾 *esoteric* = understood by only a small group

**Seldon's counter:** "Thermodynamics (热力学) is clear and true — but not everyone can design an engine. I doubt if the learned Commissioners — "

*(One Commissioner hisses something. Advocate flushes. Interrupts.)*

> 🧾 *asperity* = harshness, sharpness
> 🧾 *flushes* = face turns red (embarrassment/anger)

---

### Q4: Are you trying to destroy public confidence? (故意破坏公众信心?)

**Seldon:** "That is not so."

---

### Q5: Are you an agent for someone else?

**Seldon:** "I am not in the pay of any man."

---

### Q6: Can the future be changed?

| Advocate | Seldon |
|:---|:---|
| "Can the overall history of the human race be changed?" | "No." (then corrects) "With great difficulty." |

**The Inertia Principle** (惯性原理):

> *"The psychohistoric trend of a planet-full of people contains a **huge inertia**. To change it, you need something with **similar inertia** — either as many people, or **enormous time**."*

> 🧾 *inertia* = resistance to change

---

### Q7: Could 100,000 people change the trend?

| Seldon's Answer | Why |
|:---|:---|
| "No. Far too few." | Trantor population = **40+ billion** |
| The trend belongs to the **whole Empire** | Empire population = **nearly a quintillion** (百万的四次方) |

> 🧾 *quintillion* = 1 followed by 18 zeros

**Advocate's deduction:** "Then your 100,000 **cannot** prevent the destruction of Trantor."

**Seldon:** "Unfortunately correct."

**Advocate:** "Then they are intended for no *illegal* purpose?"

**Seldon:** "Exactly."

---

## 🪤 THE TRAP SPRINGS

**Advocate:** (slowly, with satisfaction)

> *"What **is** the purpose of your hundred thousand?"*

The room buzzes. Peers whisper. Commissioners sway toward each other.

*Only the Chief remains unmoved.*

> 🧾 *strident* = loud and harsh
> 🧾 *astutely* = cleverly, sharply

---

## 🎤 Seldon's Answer

> *"To **minimize the effects** of that destruction."*

---

## 💥 The Bombshell

**Seldon explains:**

> *"The coming destruction of Trantor is not an isolated event. It is the climax to an intricate drama that began centuries ago — the **developing decline and fall of the Galactic Empire**."*

The room becomes a **dull roar**. Cries of **"TREASON!"** from the audience.

*Gavel drops. Gong sounds. Silence.*

> 🧾 *intricate* = complex, complicated
> 🧾 *decline* = gradual decrease/weakening

---

## 📉 The Empire: Not as Strong as It Looks

| Advocate | Seldon |
|:---|:---|
| "The Empire has stood for 12,000 years!" | "I know." |
| "It has the love of a quadrillion humans!" | "I know." |
| "You predict its ruin?" | "Mathematics predicts it. I pass no moral judgment." |

> 🧾 *quadrillion* = 1 followed by 15 zeros
> 🧾 *moral judgement* = deciding right/wrong

---

### The Rotten Tree Analogy 🌳💨

> *"The rotten tree-trunk, until the very moment the storm-blast breaks it in two, has all the appearance of might it ever had. The storm-blast whistles through the branches of the Empire **even now**. Listen with the ears of psychohistory, and you will hear the **creaking**."*

> 🧾 *creaking* = squeaking sound of old wood about to break

---

## ⏳ What Comes After

| Advocate | Seldon |
|:---|:---|
| "Forever?" | "No. But the dark ages will last **30,000 years**. A Second Empire will rise — but between it and us: **one thousand generations of suffering humanity**." |

---

## 💡 The Goal: Shorten the Darkness

> *"We cannot prevent the fall. But we can **shorten the interregnum** (the gap between empires) to a **single millennium** — if we act now."*

> 🧾 *interregnum* = period between two reigns/empires

---

### How?

**By saving knowledge.**

> *"With the destruction of our social fabric, science will be broken into a million pieces. Bits of lore — meaningless alone — will be lost through generations."*

**The Solution:**

> *"We are preparing a **giant summary of all knowledge** — the **Encyclopedia Galactica**. By the time Trantor falls, it will be complete. Copies will exist in every major library in the Galaxy."*

---

## 🎭 The Show Ends

**Seldon leaves the stand.** Sits next to Gaal. Smiles.

**Seldon:** "How did you like the show?"

**Gaal:** "You stole it. What will happen now?"

**Seldon:** "They'll adjourn the trial and try to reach a private agreement with me."

**Gaal:** "How do you know?"

**Seldon:** (honest) "I don't *know*. It depends on the Chief Commissioner. I've studied him for years — but individual vagaries are risky in psychohistoric equations. Yet... **I have hopes.** "

> 🧾 *adjourn* = pause, stop temporarily
> 🧾 *vagaries* = unpredictable changes, whims

## 💭 Think About This

Seldon just told the Empire — to its face — that it's *dying*.

He called its strength an *illusion*.

He said its fall will bring **30,000 years of darkness**.

And then he smiled and asked Gaal if he liked the *show*.

*Is he brave — or has he already calculated every possible outcome?* 🤔
