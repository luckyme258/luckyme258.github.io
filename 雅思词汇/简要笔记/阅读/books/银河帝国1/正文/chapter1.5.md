# ⛓️ Chapter — Arrested

---

## 😴 Wake-Up Call (The Wrong Kind)

**The next morning.** A muted buzzer.

Gaal answers. The desk clerk's voice — *muted, polite, deprecating*:

> *"You are under detention at the orders of the **Commission of Public Safety**."*

> 🧾 *muted* = quiet, softened
> 🧾 *deprecating* = apologetic, showing regret
> 🧾 *detention* = being held/prisoner

**Gaal springs to the door.** It won't open.

*He can only dress and wait.*

---

## 🗣️ The Questioning

*Civilized. Polite. Gentle.*

| They asked | Gaal answered |
|:---|:---|
| Where from? | Synnax (provincial) |
| Education? | Such and such schools |
| Degree? | Doctor of Mathematics |
| Why here? | Job on Seldon's staff |

*Over and over.* Same questions. Same answers.

Then they circled back:

- How did you hear about the Seldon Project?
- What are your duties?
- What secret instructions?
- **What is it all about?**

**Gaal:** "I don't know. No secret instructions. I'm a scholar. A mathematician. No interest in politics."

> 🧾 *inquisitor* = questioner (especially in an investigation)

---

## 💣 The Real Question

The gentle inquisitor leans in:

> *"When will Trantor be destroyed?"*

**Gaal falters:** "I could not say of my own knowledge."

"Could you say of anyone's?"

"How could I speak for another?"

*He feels warm. Overwarm.*

> 🧾 *falters* = hesitates, speaks uncertainly

---

## 🕵️ The Trap Springs

**Inquisitor:** "You have been followed, doctor."

- At the airport when you arrived ✈️
- On the observation tower when you waited 👀
- We overheard your conversation with Dr. Seldon 🎤

> 🧾 *overhear* = hear without the speaker's knowledge

**Gaal:** "Then you know his views."

"Perhaps. But we would like to hear them from **you**."

---

### Gaal's Testimony

| Question | Gaal's Answer |
|:---|:---|
| What does Seldon think? | Trantor destroyed within **three centuries** |
| Did he prove it — *mathematically*? | "Yes, he did." (defiantly) |
| Do you think the math is *valid*? | "If Dr. Seldon vouches for it, it is valid." |

> 🧾 *defiantly* = showing resistance, not backing down
> 🧾 *vouches for* = guarantees, stands behind

**Inquisitor:** "Then we will return."

**Gaal:** "Wait! I have a right to a lawyer. I demand my rights as an Imperial citizen!"

**Inquisitor:** "You shall have them."

---

## ⚖️ The Lawyer

---

### First Impressions

| Appearance | Feeling |
|:---|:---|
| Tall | Face: all vertical lines |
| Thin | "Could there be room for a smile?" |
| Name: **Lors Avakim** | Sent by **Dr. Seldon** |

> 🧾 *disheveled* = messy, untidy
> 🧾 *wilted* = drooping, exhausted

**Gaal:** (exploding) "I demand an instant appeal to the Emperor! I'm being held without cause! I'm innocent of *anything*!"

---

### Avakim's Reality Check

> *"The Commission will have a spy beam on our conversation. Against the law — but they'll use it anyway."*

**But** — Avakim's recorder has a secret: it **blankets the spy beam**. They won't find out immediately.

> 🧾 *blankets* = covers up, blocks

**Gaal:** "Then I *can* speak?"

**Avakim:** "Of course."

**Gaal:** "Then I want a hearing with the Emperor!"

---

### The Cold Truth 🥶

**Avakim smiles** — *frostily*. (Yes, there *is* room for a smile on that thin face. His cheeks wrinkle to make room.)

> *"You are from the provinces."*

**Gaal:** "I am still an Imperial citizen!"

**Avakim:** "No doubt. It's just that — as a provincial — you don't understand life on Trantor."

> *"There are **no** hearings before the Emperor."*

| What Gaal thinks | What's real |
|:---|:---|
| Appeal to Emperor | Not possible |
| Legal recourse | None (practically) |
| The Emperor today | Not like the old dynasties |
| Who really rules | **Aristocratic families** — they *are* the Commission |

> 🧾 *recourse* = option for help
> 🧾 *legalistically* = by strict law (but not in reality)

**Avakim adds:** "This is a development well predicted by psychohistory."

---

## 😤 Gaal's Frustration

> *"If Seldon can predict history 300 years ahead — why couldn't he predict THIS MORNING and warn me?"*

*(Beat)*

"I'm sorry. I understand. Psychohistory can't predict one man's future. I'm just upset."

> 🧾 *upset* = distressed, emotional

---

### But Avakim Says:

> *"Dr. Seldon **was** of the opinion that you would be arrested this morning."*

**Gaal:** "WHAT?!"

---

## 🎭 The Truth Comes Out

| Reason | Explanation |
|:---|:---|
| Commission's hostility | Growing against Seldon |
| New members | Interfered with more and more |
| The graphs | Showed a climax was needed *now* |
| Commission's speed | Too slow |
| Why Seldon visited you yesterday | **To force their hand** — no other reason |

> 🧾 *climax* = peak, decisive moment
> 🧾 *force their hand* = make them act sooner than they planned

**Gaal:** "I *resent* — "

**Avakim:** "Please. You weren't picked for personal reasons."

---

## 📊 The Probabilities

> *"Dr. Seldon's plans — developed over 18 years of mathematics — include **all** eventualities with significant probabilities."*

| Outcome | Probability |
|:---|:---|
| Project succeeds | **> 99.9%** |
| Gaal's personal safety | **77.2%** |
| Gaal gets prison/death | < 22.8% |
| Death specifically | **< 1%** |

> 🧾 *eventualities* = possible future events

**Gaal:** "Calculations upon one man mean nothing. Send me Dr. Seldon!"

**Avakim:** "Unfortunately, I cannot. **Dr. Seldon is himself arrested.** "

---

## 🚪 The Door Bursts Open

A guard enters. Walks to the table. Picks up the recorder. Looks at it from all sides. Puts it in his pocket.

**Avakim:** (quietly) "I will need that instrument."

**Guard:** "We will supply you with one, Counsellor, that does **not** cast a static field."

**Avakim:** "My interview is done."

He leaves.

*Gaal watches him go.*

*Alone.*

## 💭 Think About This

Seldon knew Gaal would be arrested. He used him — without telling him.

Seldon's own chance of execution? **1.7%** (from previous chapter).

The project's chance of success? **>99.9%**.

*Is Seldon a hero — or a cold mathematician who sees people as numbers?* 🤔
