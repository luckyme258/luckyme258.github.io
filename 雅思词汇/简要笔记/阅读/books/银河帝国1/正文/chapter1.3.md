# 🌆 TRANTOR — The Metal World

---

## 📖 Encyclopedia Galactica Entry

> *At the beginning of the thirteenth millennium, this tendency reached its climax.*

**The Capital Planet — By the Numbers**

| 📊 Statistic | Value |
|:---|:---|
| Land surface | 75 million sq miles |
| Urbanization | 100% — *one single city* |
| Population (peak) | 40+ **billion** |
| Agricultural worlds feeding it | 20 |
| Ships landing daily | Tens of thousands |

> 🧾 *climax* = highest point
> 🧾 *urbanization* = becoming a city
> 🧾 *administrative* = related to governing

---

**The Big Problem**

- Dependence on outer worlds for **everything** (food, supplies, life itself)
- Result: vulnerable to **siege** (包围攻击)
- Last millennium: endless **revolts**
- Imperial policy = protect Trantor's **"jugular vein"** (致命弱点)

> 🧾 *vulnerable* = easy to attack
> 🧾 *siege* = surrounding a city to cut off supplies
> 🧾 *revolts* = rebellions
> 🧾 *jugular vein* = metaphor for a critical, exposed weakness

---

## 🏨 Gaal at the Luxor Hotel

---

### First Confusion

- ❓ Sun shining? No idea.
- ❓ Day or night? No clue.
- 🌞 Found the "Sun Room" — *fake sun* (artificial radiation)

> 🧾 *partaken* = eaten
> 🧾 *artificial* = man-made, not real

---

### Asking for a Tour

| Gaal's Question | Answer |
|:---|:---|
| "Where can I buy a ticket for a planetary tour?" | "Right here." |
| "When will it start?" | "You just missed it. Next one tomorrow." |

*Tomorrow = too late. He has to be at the University.*

"An observation tower? In the *open air*?"

"Sure. Let me check the weather." ☀️

> 🧾 *observation tower* = a tall building to see the view

---

### The Clerk's Attitude

- "Good weather. Dry season, I think."
- "Last time I was outside? **Three years ago.** "
- "You see it once, that's all there is to it."

> 🧾 *conversationally* = in a casual, chatty way

---

## 🚪 The Tower Elevator

---

### New Technology

| Feature | Detail |
|:---|:---|
| Type | Gravitic repulsion (反重力) |
| Sensation | Suspended in space → weightless → feet leave floor |

> 🧾 *gravitic repulsion* = technology that pushes against gravity

---

### Awkward Moment 😅

- Elevator accelerates → Gaal's feet leave the floor
- He *squawks* (unintentionally — embarrassing)
- Operator: "Tuck your feet under the railing! Can't you read the sign?"
- Everyone else: already positioned correctly
- Gaal: scrambling, failing
- A stranger pulls him down

> 🧾 *squawked* = made a sharp, surprised cry (like a bird)
> 🧾 *clamber* = climb awkwardly

---

## 🌄 The View from the Tower

---

**First Impression**

> *He stepped out upon an open terrace bathed in a white brilliance that hurt his eyes.*

- Mouth open. Gaping. 🤤
- A man (Jerril) guides him to a seat.

> 🧾 *terrace* = flat outdoor area
> 🧾 *brilliance* = intense brightness
> 🧾 *gaping* = staring with mouth open

---

### What Gaal Saw

| He could NOT see | He could see |
|:---|:---|
| The ground | Metal against sky |
| Horizon (normal) | Uniform grayness — *all over the planet* |
| Motion | A few pleasure-craft |
| Green / soil / life (except man) | — |

> 🧾 *panorama* = a wide, unbroken view
> 🧾 *pleasure-craft* = small vehicles for fun

---

**The Emperor's Palace** 👑

- Location: somewhere on Trantor
- Size: 100 square miles
- Features: natural soil, green trees, rainbow flowers
- But: *an island in an ocean of steel*
- From here? Not visible. Might be 10,000 miles away.

---

### Gaal's Feeling

> *He saw none of its weaknesses.*

- No food ships landing
- No awareness of the "jugular vein"
- Only saw: **the mightiest deed of man** — complete conquest of a world

> 🧾 *mightiest* = most powerful
> 🧾 *contemptuously* = showing disrespect (as if it was easy)

---

## 💬 Conversation with Jerril

---

### First Impressions

| Jerril | Gaal |
|:---|:---|
| "First time on Trantor?" | "Yes." |
| "Trantor gets you if you've got the poetic temperament." | — |
| "Trantorians never come up here. Gives them nerves." | "Glorious!" |

> 🧾 *temperament* = personality type
> 🧾 *nerves* = anxiety, fear

---

### Why Trantorians Avoid the Open Sky

> *"If you're born in a cubicle and grow up in a corridor, and work in a cell, and vacation in a crowded sun-room — then coming up into the open with nothing but sky over you might give you a nervous breakdown."*

- Children: forced to come up once a year (after age 5)
- First few times: *scream themselves into hysteria* 😱
- Jerril's suggestion: start as soon as they're weaned, go once a week

> 🧾 *cubicle* = small room/workspace
> 🧾 *hysteria* = uncontrollable emotion (fear/crying)
> 🧾 *weaned* = stopped breastfeeding

---

### How High?

| Gaal's guess | Actual height |
|:---|:---|
| Half a mile | **500 feet** |

"But the elevator took —"

"Most of that time was getting up to *ground level*. Trantor is tunneled over a mile down."

> 🧾 *tunneled* = dug with tunnels

---

### Trantor's Energy

- Old way: atomic generators
- New way: temperature difference between ground level and depths (cheaper)

> 🧾 *atomic generators* = nuclear power plants

---

### Jerril's Questions (Getting Sly)

- "What do you think of it all?" → "Glorious."
- "Here on vacation? Traveling?" → "No — a job."
- "With Dr. Seldon's project." → *Interest intensifies*

> 🧾 *shrewdness* = sharp, clever awareness
> 🧾 *sly* = secretive, knowing

---

## 🧠 The Name "Raven"

| Gaal | Jerril |
|:---|:---|
| "Hari Seldon." | "Raven Seldon?" |
| "No — Hari Seldon. The psychohistorian." | "Same guy. They call him Raven. Slang. He keeps predicting disaster." |

**Gaal:** *Genuinely astonished.* "He does?"

**Jerril:** "You're coming to work for him, aren't you?"

**Gaal:** "I've only read his mathematical papers."

**Jerril:** "Yes, *the ones they publish.*" (😏 implication: there's more he's NOT publishing)

> 🧾 *Raven* = a black bird associated with doom/bad omens
> 🧾 *astonished* = very surprised

---

### Gaal Leaves

- Slightly annoyed
- "I think I'll go to my room now. Pleased to have met you."
- Jerril waves indifferently

> 🧾 *indifferently* = without caring

---

## 🚪 The Surprise in His Room

---

**Scene:** Gaal opens the door. A man is waiting inside.

| Gaal | The Man |
|:---|:---|
| Startled | Old, almost bald |
| "What are you doing here?" (unsaid) | Walks with a limp |
| — | Eyes: very bright and blue |

> 🧾 *startled* = suddenly surprised
> 🧾 *limp* = difficulty walking (injury)

---

### The Introduction

> *"I am Hari Seldon."*

— an instant before Gaal's befuddled brain placed the face alongside the memory of the many times he had seen it in pictures.

> 🧾 *befuddled* = confused, unable to think clearly
